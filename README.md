# eyebrowse

A Chrome extension for hands-free short-form video browsing. Scroll Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and TikTok using your eyes and like videos by shaking your head—zero mouse or keyboard input required.

## Features

- **Eye-Tracking Scroll:** Tracks vertical eyebrow movement shifts via your webcam to automatically scroll to the next or previous video in your feed.
- **Head-Shake to Like:** Detects quick horizontal head shakes to trigger a like hands-free.
- **Multi-Platform Support:** Works natively across Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and TikTok web tabs.
- **100% Local & Private:** Runs real-time facial landmark detection locally inside the extension—no camera footage ever leaves your browser.
- **Extension Toolbar Controls:** Toggle tracking on/off, recalibrate your neutral gaze, and adjust sensitivity directly from the extension popup.

## Installation

1. Clone or download this repository to your computer.
2. Open Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions`.
3. Turn on **Developer mode** in the top-right corner.
4. Click **Load unpacked** and select the `eyebrowse` project folder.
5. Open Reels, Shorts, or TikTok, click the **eyebrowse** icon in your Chrome toolbar to grant camera access, and start browsing hands-free.
